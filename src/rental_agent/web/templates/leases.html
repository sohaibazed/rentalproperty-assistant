Move-out and security deposit

When tenants leave, Texas law gives you {{ deadline_days }} days from the day they move out to refund the deposit and, if you keep any of it, to send an itemized list of the deductions. The duty starts once the tenant gives a forwarding address in writing. This page sends nothing and moves no money: you mail the letter and the refund.
